   * Custom domain or sub-domain: `doctoratetodata.substack.com`.
   * Clear tagline: *"Weekly Python code tutorials, job market data reports, and honest notes on transitioning from STEM academia to Data Science."*
2. **Bio Link Integration:**
   * Place Substack link at the top of your TikTok bio link-in-bio (e.g., Beacons, Linktree, or direct Substack URL).
3. **In-Video Call to Action (CTA):**
   * Mention in videos: *"Full step-by-step code tutorial and dataset download available on my Substack—link in bio!"*
